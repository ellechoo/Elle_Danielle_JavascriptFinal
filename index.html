InterFACE Tracker

How to Use:

    As you surf the web, interFace detects your facial expressions in real time, revealing how your mood shifts across the digital world.

  • Download and turn on the InterFACE chrome extension.
  • Allow webcam access for this website.
  • Just keep this tab open and use the web like you normally would.
  • To check your facial expressions while on other tabs, click on the InterFACE chrome extension for a live popup.
  • Scroll down on this page to see a summary of your emotions over a period of time.
  • Note: Good lighting is recommended for accurate results.
